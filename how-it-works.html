Role-Based Step-by-Step Guide

Detailed Role Workflows

Mess Manager (Admin)

Admin Setup & Management Flow

01

Sign In & Choose "Create Mess"

Sign into Messliner and select "Create New Mess" in the onboarding wizard.

02

Set Mess Name & Tracked Meals

Input your mess name and pick active meal times (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).

03

Set Rate Mode, Cutoffs & Rules

Choose Market Ratio or Fixed Rate, set meal lock cutoff times, and agree to rules.

04

Get 6-Digit Code & Launch

System generates unique 6-digit Mess Code. Share with housemates and launch database.

05

Manage Bazar & Member Deposits

Log daily market expenses with receipt photos and record verified member deposits.

Mess Member (User)

Member Join & Daily Meal Flow

01

Sign Up & Profile Setup

Register with your email and password, then access your member portal.

02

Select "Join Existing Mess"

Choose "Join Existing Mess" option in your onboarding wizard screen.

03

Input 6-Digit Invite Code

Enter the 6-digit mess code provided by your Manager for instant match.

04

Toggle Meals & Track Balance

Turn meals ON/OFF before lock deadlines and view live meal rate & deposit ledger.
