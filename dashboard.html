Frequently Asked Questions

6 Guides
How do I record or edit my daily meals?
Go to the My Meal tab in the sidebar navigation. There you can set your Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner counts for today and upcoming dates before your mess manager's cutoff time.
How is the Meal Rate automatically calculated?
Meal Rate is calculated with full transparency: Meal Rate = Total Bazar Expenses ÷ Total Consumed Meals across all active dining members.
How do I deposit money into my mess account?
Send your deposit money (via bKash, Nagad, or Cash) to your dining meal manager. Once received, the manager enters it in the Finance / Deposits section, and your balance updates instantly in your Profile.
How do new members join our mess?
Open the Profile page to copy your unique 6-character Mess Code (e.g. DD-HE2XR). When your roommates register on Messliner, they enter this code to join your mess dining.
How do I change my password or profile name?
Go to Profile from the top right avatar, click on your avatar to edit your display name and phone number, or click Change Password under Security & Access.
What should I do if I face an issue or calculation bug?
Use the Report or Suggestion form on this page or message us directly on WhatsApp at 01309968240. Our support team responds promptly to ensure your dining calculations remain accurate.

Messliner Dining Management Platform Agreement

1. Usage of Platform Messliner is designed for mess managers and members to track daily meals, shared bazaar expenses, deposits, and financial accounts transparently.
2. User Responsibility Managers and members are responsible for ensuring accurate transaction and meal entries. All operations perform real-time calculation in accordance with mess configuration.
3. Data & Security Account authentication and mess records are stored securely in Google Firebase. You are responsible for keeping your credentials safe.

Privacy Policy

How your personal and dining data is protected

1. Information We Collect We collect basic identity info (name, email, phone number) and dining entries (meals, deposits, shared bills, and bazaar costs) to run your mess seamlessly.
2. Access Controls Only authenticated members of your dining mess can view mess balances and statistics. Managers have administrative permissions to oversee records.
3. No Third-Party Selling Your mess data is strictly yours and is never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.
